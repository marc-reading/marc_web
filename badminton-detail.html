MARC Open Badminton Tournament 2026 (Intermediate Level)

Organised by the Malayalee Association of Reading Community (MARC), this tournament reflects MARC’s commitment to community building, inclusivity, and youth engagement through cultural, social, and sporting initiatives.

A flagship MARC initiative, the UK-wide Badminton Tournament will be held on 7th March 2026 at Rivermead Leisure Centre, Reading. Building on the success of the 2025 inaugural event, the 2026 edition aims to host 120 teams and 240 participants, recognising and encouraging badminton talent from across the UK in an inclusive and competitive environment.

We wish all participants the very best and sincerely thank our sponsors and volunteers for their invaluable support. This tournament is a celebration of sport, unity, and MARC’s enduring bond with the wider community.

Sincerely,
MARC Samithi & MARC Badminton Organising Committee
