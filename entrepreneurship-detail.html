Event Details

For the Malayalee Community in the UK

This is a community-driven event created to encourage a business mindset among Malayalees living in the UK. This event brings together 7–8 successful and friendly entrepreneurs who have built businesses in the UK to share their real-life journeys, including the challenges they faced, the risks they took, the mistakes they learned from, and the breakthroughs that changed their path.

Our speakers will share the raw truth behind their journeys: the risks, the mistakes, and the breakthroughs that led to their success. Through these authentic insightful stories, open panel discussions, live Q&A and networking opportunities, attendees will gain practical insight into what it truly takes to start and grow a business in the UK. This is not a formal seminar, training course, or awards night. It is a friendly, honest, and supportive space to listen, ask questions, connect, and be inspired.

Whether you already have a business idea or are just beginning to explore the possibility, this event is designed to give you clarity, confidence, and meaningful connections to take your next step.

Come. Listen. Connect. Grow.

What to expect

  • Raw and practical lessons from real business journeys
  • Open panel discussion and Q&A
  • Insights into starting and growing a business in the UK
  • Informal networking with speakers and attendees
  • A supportive, positive, business-minded community

Who should attend

  • Professionals exploring business ideas
  • Students & career changers
  • Aspiring entrepreneurs
  • Early-stage founders
  • Anyone curious about business

About Our Speakers

Entrepreneurs and leaders sharing real journeys, lessons, and practical insights.

Founder & CEO
Envertiz Group

Febin Cyriac

Featured in Forbes for his entrepreneurial grit, Febin is the strategic mind who scaled international ventures like Visa Roots, Envertiz Academy & Consultancy, and Thejus Foods from the ground up. His business ventures and clients are now spread across Europe, Asia, and the Middle East.

CEO & CSO, Ivy Farm Technologies,
CEO Stealth mode biotech

Dr Harsh Amin, PhD

Dr Harsh represents the next generation of biotech leadership. He has guided organisations through regulatory hurdles, fundraising, and scale-up phases to reach commercial readiness, and he is a frequent voice on BBC and CNBC regarding food security and innovation.

Founder
UK-Kerala Business Forum

Philip Abraham

A distinguished public servant and former Mayor of Loughton, Philip Abraham is a pioneer for the Indian diaspora in the UK. As the founder of the UK–Kerala Business Forum, he leverages decades of civic leadership to foster international trade and empower local communities through non-political advocacy.

Founder
AyataCommerce and InvoTools

Shine Mathew

Shine is a technology leader and founder of successful global IT firms AyataCommerce and InvoTools. A client-focused, mission-driven tech entrepreneur specialising in eCommerce platforms, AI-integrated retail, and order management systems, his firms are now operating and growing worldwide.

Director of Culinary
Award Winning Celebrity Chef

Chef Jomon

Jomon is Director of Culinary for The Tiffinbox across the UK and UAE. He is one of UK's influential Malayalis, and former Executive Chef at The LaLiT London. An award winner with the Craft Guild of Chefs and known for his appearance on Celebrity MasterChef, Jomon brings together refined culinary artistry and strategic leadership.

Founder
Tutorsvalley & UK Event Life

Nordi Jacob

Nordi previously worked with the European Central Bank, BBC, and HSBC before moving into entrepreneurship. He founded Tutorsvalley and UK Event Life, using his IT expertise and business experience to build a leading personalised education platform and a growing event and ticketing platform.

Digital Influencer
Licensed Pilot

Sachin Pattathuparambil

Sachin is a licensed pilot and a rising digital influencer who is turning his passion for flying into a career beyond traditional paths. He also encourages the next generation to break boundaries, blend hobbies with careers, and aim higher with confidence.

Co-Founder
Theresa’s London

Deepa Joseph

Sisters-in-law Deepa and Deepthi share the story of women in families building a business. They founded Theresa’s London, a fast-growing online fashion outlet. Through social media, they have grown a loyal community of 40k+ followers and built a strong benchmark for customer service.
