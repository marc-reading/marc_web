Partnership & Sponsorship

MARC collaborates with partners and sponsors to deliver impactful cultural, social, and sports events that bring together Malayalee families across Reading and surrounding areas.

We are actively seeking long-term partnerships and event sponsors who wish to engage with a vibrant, family-focused community while growing their brand presence across multiple MARC initiatives.

Why partner or sponsor MARC?

  • Engagement opportunities through festivals, sports events, and family programmes
  • Consistent on-site and digital brand visibility across all MARC events
  • Active promotion via MARC’s social media and community networks
  • Association with charity work and positive social impact

If you are interested in partnering with MARC or sponsoring our events, we would be delighted to explore opportunities together.
